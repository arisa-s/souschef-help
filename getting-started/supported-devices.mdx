The full Souschef experience is available as a free app for **iOS** and **Android**. Shared recipe, shopping list, and collection links also open in a lightweight web viewer, so anyone can view them in their browser without installing the app.

## iOS (iPhone & iPad)

Souschef is available for iPhone and iPad running a recent version of iOS/iPadOS. It was the first platform Souschef launched on, so the iOS version is well-established and packed with features.

[Download on the App Store →](https://apps.apple.com/app/souschef-recipe-organizer/id6468939420)

## Web (shared recipes, lists, and collections)

Souschef includes a lightweight web experience for sharing — not a full desktop app.

When someone opens a shared **recipe** link in their browser, they can:

- View the recipe
- Read ingredients and instructions
- [Adjust serving sizes](/using/adjust-servings)
- Switch between supported [measurement systems](/using/convert-measurements) (Metric, US Customary, and more)

When someone opens a shared **shopping list** link, they can view the list. On an **edit** link, they can check items off after entering a short name. See [Share a shopping list](/using/share-shopping-list).

When someone opens a shared **collection** link, they can browse titles and cards. Joining and editing happens in the app. See [Shared collections](/using/shared-collections).

<Note>
  To import recipes, organize your library, journal, plan meals, and access the
  full feature set, use the iOS or Android app.
</Note>
