Souschef is a free app for **iOS** and **Android**. Share recipe lists with a web link so anyone can view shared recipes, shopping lists, and collections in a browser without installing the app.

## iOS (iPhone & iPad)

Souschef is available for iPhone and iPad running a recent version of iOS/iPadOS. It was the first platform Souschef launched on, so the iOS version is well-established and packed with features.

## Web (shared recipes, lists, and collections)

View shared recipe lists on the web. Share recipe lists with a web link.

When someone opens a shared **recipe** link in their browser, they can:

- View the recipe
- Read ingredients and instructions
- [Adjust serving sizes](/using/adjust-servings)
- Switch between supported [measurement systems](/using/convert-measurements) (Metric, US Customary, and more)

When someone opens a shared **shopping list** link (`trysouschef.app/s/…`), they can view the list. If **Allow editing** is on, they enter a short name, then check or uncheck items. On a view-only link, they can look but not check. See [Share a shopping list](/using/share-shopping-list).

When someone opens a shared **collection** link, they can browse recipes. They cannot edit from the web. Joining happens in the app. See [Shared collections](/using/shared-collections).

<Note>
  Importing, cooking, and organizing happen in the iOS and Android apps. On the
  web, you can view shared recipe lists.
</Note>
