Too many recipes saved in too many places? Between Instagram posts, TikTok videos, Facebook shares, YouTube, browser bookmarks, screenshots, and handwritten notes, it's easy for a great recipe to get lost. Souschef is a free recipe keeper for iOS and Android. Import recipes in a few taps, keep them organized, and cook with them. Share recipe lists with a web link so anyone can view them without installing the app.

## Where can I import recipes from?

Souschef can pull recipes from a wide range of places, including:

- **Instagram** — [posts and reels](/importing/import-from-instagram)
- **TikTok** — [recipe videos](/importing/import-from-tiktok)
- **Facebook** — [posts, videos, and reels](/importing/import-from-facebook)
- **YouTube** — [cooking videos](/importing/import-from-youtube)
- **Pinterest** — [recipe pins](/importing/import-from-pinterest)
- **Recipe websites and food blogs** — [almost any site](/importing/import-from-website)
- **Photos and screenshots** — [scan any image](/importing/import-from-photos)
- **Plain text** — [paste text or write manually](/importing/import-from-plain-text)

We're always working to expand support for more sources. If there's a platform you'd love to see added, drop us a note at [team@nekonote.co](mailto:team@nekonote.co).

## Where is Souschef available?

Souschef is available on **iOS** and **Android**:

- 🍎 **iPhone & iPad** — [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/souschef-recipe-organizer/id6468939420?utm_source=website&utm_medium=seo&utm_campaign=free_recipe_keeper&utm_content=docs_what_is_souschef)
- 🤖 **Android phones & tablets** — [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.souschef.app&utm_source=website&utm_medium=seo&utm_campaign=free_recipe_keeper&utm_content=docs_what_is_souschef)

View shared recipe lists on the web. When someone opens a shared recipe link in their browser, they can read the recipe, adjust serving sizes, and switch measurement systems. Shared shopping lists can be checked off if you allow editing. Shared collections can be browsed.

Importing, cooking, and organizing happen in the iOS or Android app. See [supported devices](/getting-started/supported-devices) for details.
