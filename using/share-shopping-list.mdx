Share a private link so friends can shop from the same live list. Links are not public. Anyone with the link can open it. People who open it in the app and sign in join your library.

One live grocery list so you’re not juggling two lists or a text thread at the store. See [Shopping List](/using/shopping-list) to build a list. After you create one, Souschef offers **Invite Friends** on the spot. Sharing uses the same private-link model as [collections](/using/shared-collections).

## What you can share

| | Collection | Shopping list |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Live share | Browse recipes together | Shop the same list together |
| Web, no account | View recipes | Check items off (if you allow editing) |
| App, signed in | Collection appears in **Bookmarks** | List appears in **Shopping** |
| Snapshot | — | Share as plain text |

## How you share

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a list, or open Share on one you own">
    Open a shopping list you own. After you create a shopping list, Souschef offers **Invite Friends** on the spot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Allow editing on or off">
    **Allow editing** on: people who join in the app become **Collaborators**. **Allow editing** off: they join as **Viewers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy or send the private link">
    Send the link however you usually share — Messages, email, or another app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The link is unlisted — it isn’t a public page. Web pages for shared lists are not meant to show up in search results.

## Open a shared link

Shared links look like `trysouschef.app/s/…`.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="On the web">
    Open the link in a browser. You don’t need an account.

    - **Allow editing** on: enter a short name, then check or uncheck items. Others see who checked what.
    - **View only**: look, don’t check.

    Web guests are not added to the app until they sign in and join.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="In the app">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Sign in">
        Open the link in Souschef. Sign in, or create an account.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Join the list">
        The list is added to **Shopping**. Your role comes from the link: editing on → **Collaborator**; off → **Viewer**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    You can rejoin later with the same link if you leave.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

If a link is off, expired, or unknown, you’ll see: **This link is no longer available. Ask the owner to send a new private link.** See [Share link unavailable](/troubleshooting/share-link-unavailable).

## Who can do what

- **You (owner)** — share, change the link type, remove people, turn sharing off, rename, and delete the list.
- **Collaborator** — add or edit items, check off, and rename. They cannot delete the list or turn sharing off.
- **Viewer** — look only. They can leave.
- **Web guest (editing on)** — check items with a name. They are not added to the app until they sign in and join.

<Note>
  View-only members can see the list but cannot shop it. Web guests must add a
  short name before checking items.
</Note>

## Use a shared list in the app

Switch lists from the title to move between lists you own and lists you joined. Shared lists show people’s avatars. Checks say who last checked them.

Owners manage people from **Share**. If you don’t own the list, you can leave it and rejoin later with the same still-active link.

## Share as plain text instead

Shopping lists only: **Share as text** sends a one-time copy. That is not live.

Use this when you need to send the list to someone who doesn’t use Souschef — sorted by aisle — via Messages or any messaging app. For a live list, use the private link above.

## Turn sharing off

The owner can turn off sharing / collaboration at any time.

<Warning>
  The list is private again. The link stops working. Everyone else loses access.
  Checked items stay on yours.
</Warning>

- **New link** — the old link dies. People who already joined keep access.
- **Remove someone** — they lose access until they use a working link again.

<Warning>
  Lists with no edits or check-offs for **7 days** are deleted — including
  shared ones. Checking something counts as activity.
</Warning>
