Souschef Plus gives you unlimited recipe imports. All cooking and organization tools are free.

Souschef is free to download from the App Store and Google Play. The free plan includes **5 saved recipe imports per rolling 7 days**. Existing saved recipes remain available even after you hit the limit.

## Cooking and organization tools are free

Cooking Mode, grocery lists, meal planning, cooking journal, collections, search, editing, and sharing are free. You can also use pinned recipes, Pantry, Nutrition, AI Tagging, AI Recipe Translation, AI Discovery, printing, serving adjustment, measurement conversion, and timers without upgrading.

Souschef Plus does **not** unlock those tools. There is no paid-only cooking feature list.

## What Souschef Plus adds

Souschef Plus gives you unlimited recipe imports:

- **Unlimited recipe imports**

On the free plan, you can still save imported recipes (5 per rolling 7 days), and existing saved recipes remain available.

## Free vs Souschef Plus

|  | Free | Souschef Plus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New recipe imports | 5 saved imports per rolling 7 days | Unlimited |
| Keep imported recipes | Yes — yours to keep | Yes — yours to keep |
| Cooking Mode, Journal, Meal Plan, Shopping Lists, Pantry, Nutrition, AI features, and more | Included | Included |

The free allowance only affects **new recipe imports**. Existing saved recipes remain available — you can still view, cook, edit, organize, and use them.

Souschef is an independently built app. Souschef Plus helps cover the cost of running recipe imports, keeps the free plan available for everyone, and supports ongoing improvements.

<Info>
  Free users can save **5 imported recipes per rolling 7 days**. Once you have used them, wait for imports to refresh or [upgrade to Souschef Plus](/billing/subscribe) for unlimited imports.
</Info>
