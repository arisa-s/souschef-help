On the free plan, you get **5 recipe imports per rolling 7 days**. An import counts toward that limit only when you tap **Save**. Reviewing a recipe and leaving without saving does not use a slot.

Once you have used all 5, you cannot import any more recipes until a slot frees up or you [upgrade to Souschef Plus](/billing/subscription-benefits).

## When an import counts

After you paste a link, share a post, scan a photo, or paste text, Souschef shows a preview. You can check the title, ingredients, and steps before you decide.

- **Saving uses one import.** Tap **Save** to add the recipe to your library. That uses one of your 5 weekly imports.
- **Leaving without saving does not count.** Close the preview or go back, and that attempt does not use a slot.
- **Failed imports do not count.** If Souschef cannot extract a recipe, you do not lose a slot. See [why imports sometimes fail](/importing/why-import-fails).

This applies to every [supported source](/importing/supported-sources).

## What happens when you have used all 5 imports

After you save 5 recipes in the current 7-day window, you cannot import any more recipes. The next time you try, the app shows a message and the import does not start.

You can still:

- View, organize, edit, search, and cook from every recipe already in your library
- Wait until a slot frees up — 7 days after an earlier saved import
- [Upgrade to Souschef Plus](/billing/subscription-benefits) for unlimited imports

See [Recipe import limits](/billing/import-limits) for how the rolling 7-day window works.

## Souschef Plus

Souschef Plus removes the import limit. You can save as many recipes as you like, with no weekly cap.

- **\$2.99 USD/month**
- **\$24.99 USD/year**

See the [Souschef Plus](/billing/subscription-benefits) page for details, or [subscribe](/billing/subscribe) from the App Store or Google Play.
