Common questions about the free plan, [Souschef Plus](/billing/subscription-benefits), and billing. If your question isn't answered here, [reach out to support](/troubleshooting/contact-support).

## About the free plan and Souschef Plus

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Souschef free?">
    Yes. All cooking and organization tools are free. Free users get 5 saved recipe imports per rolling 7 days. Souschef Plus is optional and adds unlimited imports.

    Cooking Mode, grocery lists, meal planning, cooking journal, collections, search, editing, and sharing are free. Existing saved recipes remain available even after you hit the limit.

    See [Free vs Souschef Plus](/billing/free-plan) for a complete comparison.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is Souschef Plus?">
    Souschef Plus gives you unlimited recipe imports. All cooking and organization tools are free.

    Souschef is an independently built app. Souschef Plus helps cover the cost of running recipe imports, keeps the free plan available for everyone, and supports ongoing improvements. See [About Souschef Plus](/billing/subscription-benefits) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does Souschef Plus include?">
    Souschef Plus includes unlimited recipe imports. It does not unlock separate cooking tools; those are free.

    Cooking Mode, grocery lists, meal planning, cooking journal, collections, search, editing, and sharing are free.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I still use saved recipes if I do not pay?">
    Yes. Your saved recipes and cooking tools remain available.

    Existing saved recipes remain available even after you hit the limit. You can still view, cook, edit, organize, and use them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much does Souschef Plus cost?">
    - **\$2.99 USD/month**
    - **\$24.99 USD/year**

    Both plans include the same features. You can cancel anytime.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens after I use my 5 imports?">
    You can still view, cook, edit, organize, and use existing saved recipes. You just need to wait for imports to refresh or upgrade to Plus for unlimited imports.

    The limit only affects **new recipe imports**. Free users can save 5 imported recipes per rolling 7 days. Once you've saved 5 recipes in that window, you cannot import any more until a slot frees up, or you can upgrade to [Souschef Plus](/billing/subscription-benefits) for unlimited imports.

    See [Recipe import limits](/billing/import-limits) for more detail, including [how quota is used](/billing/how-import-quota-works) when you save a recipe.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does previewing a recipe use an import?">
    No. An import counts toward your free limit only when you tap **Save**. You can review the preview and leave without saving, and that attempt does not use a slot.

    Failed imports do not count either. Once you have saved 5 recipes in the current 7-day window, you cannot import any more until a slot frees up or you upgrade to [Souschef Plus](/billing/subscription-benefits).

    See [How import quota works](/billing/how-import-quota-works).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Managing your subscription

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I cancel anytime?">
    Yes. You can cancel Souschef Plus at any time through your phone's app store settings — there are no cancellation fees. See [How to cancel](/billing/cancel) for step-by-step instructions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I cancel?">
    You'll keep Souschef Plus (unlimited imports) until the end of your current billing period. After that, your account returns to the free plan — 5 saved recipe imports per rolling 7 days.

    Your recipes stay yours to keep. You can continue viewing, organizing, editing, searching, and cooking from everything already in your library. See [what happens after you cancel](/billing/after-cancellation) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will I lose my recipes?">
    No. Cancelling Souschef Plus does not delete any of your saved recipes. Every recipe you import is yours to keep — your library stays intact and fully accessible on the free plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I restore my purchase?">
    If Souschef Plus isn't active after reinstalling or switching devices, you can restore it through the App Store or Google Play. See [Restore your purchase](/billing/restore-purchase) for the exact steps.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get a refund?">
    Refund requests go through Apple or Google, since that's where Souschef Plus is billed. See [Refunds](/billing/refund) for how to submit a request.

## Billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who processes my payment?">
    Apple (App Store) on iPhone and iPad, and Google (Google Play) on Android. Souschef does not process payments directly — your payment method, billing history, and renewal settings live inside your Apple or Google account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    No. Souschef Plus does not include a free trial. You can use the free plan to import recipes and try the app, then subscribe if you want unlimited imports.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a family plan?">
    No. Souschef Plus is an individual subscription and does not offer a family plan or shared access across Family Sharing / family library accounts. Each person who wants Plus needs their own subscription.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
