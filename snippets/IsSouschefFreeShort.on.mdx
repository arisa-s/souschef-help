Yes! Souschef is free to download, and every feature is available on the free plan. Nothing is locked behind a paywall.

Free users can import up to **5 recipes each week**. Every recipe you import is yours to keep — recipes don't expire, and the weekly limit only affects new imports.

[Souschef Plus](/billing/subscription-benefits) only removes the weekly import limit.

Souschef Plus also helps support ongoing development of the app.

Cooking Mode, Shopping Lists, Pantry, Nutrition, AI Tagging, AI Recipe Translation, AI Discovery, recipe organization, and every other feature are available on the free plan.

See [Free vs Souschef Plus](/billing/free-plan) for a complete comparison.
