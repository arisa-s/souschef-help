## Free plan import limit

Free users can save **5 imported recipes per rolling 7 days**. A recipe only counts toward that limit when you tap **Save** — previewing without saving does not use a slot. Existing saved recipes remain available even after you hit the limit. You can still view, cook, edit, organize, and use them.

Once you have used all 5, you cannot import any more recipes until a slot frees up. [Souschef Plus](/billing/subscription-benefits) gives you unlimited recipe imports. See [How import quota works](/billing/how-import-quota-works) and [Recipe import limits](/billing/import-limits) for details.
